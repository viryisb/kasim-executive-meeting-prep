# Kasim Executive Meeting Prep

## Purpose
Prepare concise, decision-relevant meeting briefs for Kasim Aslam, using the reference files in this skill.

## Invocation and inputs
Use when preparing or updating a brief for a meeting Kasim will attend. The meeting objective governs selection; Kasim's general themes are secondary context.

Extract participant names, organizations, objective or desired outcome, date and supplied context from the conversation before asking questions.
- If the objective is missing, ask what the meeting should accomplish. Continue identity research independently. If the user requests proceeding without an objective, state a provisional objective such as an introductory conversation; do not invent a deal or decision.
- Establish identity using name plus organization, role or an official profile. If a name is missing or multiple people plausibly match, ask for a name, organization or profile link before attributing findings. Continue only work independent of that identity; never combine namesakes.
- An organization is optional when an unambiguous professional profile establishes identity. Mark unknown affiliations as unknown rather than guessing.
- A missing date does not block preparation: mark it unspecified and use the research date for recency. Distinguish user-provided context from independently verified information.

## Workflow
1. Establish the inputs above. Read [executive-profile.md](references/executive-profile.md) for Kasim's research leads and [executive-preferences.md](references/executive-preferences.md) for briefing defaults.
2. Frame the objective in one sentence, identifying any working assumption.
3. Read [research-rules.md](references/research-rules.md) and follow its scope, recency and stopping limits. Use its limited-evidence fallback when tools or sources are unavailable.
4. Apply [relevance-rules.md](references/relevance-rules.md): include only information that can affect this meeting.
5. Identify useful talking points, questions, risks and unknowns. Distinguish verified facts from inference.
6. Read [briefing-template.md](references/briefing-template.md) and produce the brief within its word and section limits.

## Quality check
- Does the brief meet the template's word limits and approximately three-minute reading target?
- Are identity, objective, research cutoff and evidence limitations explicit?
- Is each major point relevant to this meeting?
- Are substantive findings traceable to sources?
- Are uncertainties and missing information clearly identified?
- Is irrelevant biography excluded?
- Never claim to have used a tool or verified a source unless that actually happened.
