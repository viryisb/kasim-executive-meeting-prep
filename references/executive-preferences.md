# Executive Preferences — Working Assumptions

These are default briefing standards for this skill, not confirmed personal preferences of Kasim. Explicit user instructions for the meeting override these defaults. Treat supplied preferences as user-provided context unless confirmation is established; do not infer private preferences from public themes.

- Lead with decision-relevant information.
- Keep background concise.
- Explain why a fact matters for the specific meeting.
- Separate facts from interpretation.
- Highlight meaningful unknowns.
- Follow the word and section limits in `briefing-template.md` for an approximately three-minute brief.
- Avoid irrelevant personal details, generic company descriptions and unverified claims.
