# Executive Meeting Brief

## Writing limits
Target 350-500 words; cap the brief at 550 words including metadata and headings, excluding the source list. Shorter is preferable when evidence is sparse. Keep the source list to 120 words with compact titles. User-requested length overrides these defaults.

Section limits below are ceilings, not quotas. Avoid repeating findings; merge background or developments into Need to know when clearer. Omit optional sections without material content. Keep Meeting, Executive summary, Suggested questions, Risks and unknowns, and Sources. If no sources were consulted, say so. Do not pad with generic advice or fabricated certainty.

## Meeting
Date (or unspecified), participants, organization (or unknown), objective and research cutoff. Mark provisional objectives and unresolved identities. Include a one-sentence limited-evidence notice when applicable. Maximum 60 words.

## Executive summary
Up to 3 bullets, maximum 90 words total. Lead with what Kasim needs to accomplish, understand or decide. Label recommendations and inferences.

## Need to know
Up to 3 findings, maximum 120 words total. Connect each fact to its meeting implication; cite sources adjacent to claims.

## Person and company snapshot (optional)
Maximum 40 words. Only essential professional context not already covered.

## Recent developments (optional)
Up to 2 dated developments, maximum 50 words total, with sources and meeting relevance.

## Talking points (optional)
Up to 2 objective-linked points, maximum 40 words total. Avoid repeating the questions.

## Suggested questions
Up to 3 specific questions, maximum 60 words total, ordered by usefulness to the objective.

## Risks and unknowns
Up to 3 items, maximum 60 words total. Distinguish documented issues from missing evidence or inference; identify the question or check needed to resolve material gaps. If no material issue emerged, qualify that statement by the research scope; do not assert an absence of risk.

## Sources
Compact numbered entries matching citations: title/publisher, link or supplied-document locator, and publication date (or undated with access date). Follow `research-rules.md`; include only consulted sources supporting the brief.
